COLD TRANSFER / FICTIONAL CASE

The fountain closure

Before the class discussion, decide what you would report. Work alone. Use only the three notes below.

  1. A neighbourhood post says, “They shut the fountain because the water failed a safety test.” Another account repeats it and links back to the same post.
  2. The city works log lists a valve replacement at the fountain today. It records no water test result.
  3. A library worker says, “I saw the public works crew put a barrier around the fountain this morning.”

Write three lines

  1. What would you report now? Write one sentence.
  2. What is still unconfirmed, and why don’t the two posts settle it?
  3. Name one specific thing you would check before reporting the safety claim as fact.

You do not need to decide whether the water is actually safe.
