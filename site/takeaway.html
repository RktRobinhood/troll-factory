CLASSROOM TAKEAWAY / CASE 01

What can we honestly report?

Three useful terms

  • Provenance: where a claim, image or document came from.
  • Independent corroboration: a separate source supports the same detail without copying the first.
  • Unresolved: there is not enough evidence for or against the claim. It does not mean false.

Check the claim, not the whole source

  1. Find the original. What did the crop or caption leave out?
  2. Check one detail against an independent source. Ask what each person actually saw.
  3. Say what you know and what you do not. Correct errors visibly.

Example: a venue notice and a separate room check support a new location. Neither proves why the venue changed.

Write this in your notes

Name one detail you could publish, one you couldn’t, and what evidence would change your mind about the second.
