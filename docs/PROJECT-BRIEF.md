# Project brief

Help upper-secondary students, including IB Theory of Knowledge, separate evidence from reach and choose what to say under uncertainty. This is an original educational project informed by Yle's 2019 newsgame, not a restoration or adaptation of its files.

## Pilot

One original civic event, conflicting accounts, a newsroom evidence desk, a justified publishing decision, concrete fictional consequences and an opportunity to correct or update. Finish with a short transfer case and a teacher discussion. Learning evidence comes from what students can justify, not from clicks, speed, political agreement or attention.

## Non-goals

No real-world posting, targeting, persuasion optimization, multiplayer, account, leaderboards, advertising, gradebook, analytics, paid assets, or student personal information. The original’s disturbing real-world material and visual branding are not reused.

## Release bar

Walk all meaningful decision branches and at least one consequence-repair path; check source comparison and uncertain vs false distinctions; exercise keyboard-only and phone navigation; test a first session and repeat session. Publish the scenario code/static assets only. Keep an honest line between simulated feedback, teacher observation, and learning effectiveness. No pedagogical efficacy claim before a classroom pilot.
