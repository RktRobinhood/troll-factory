# Historical baseline

## Observed design

Yle's 2019 mobile-first educational newsgame put the player inside a staged information operation. Scripted chat and media items led to decisions, a simulated flow of attention and a concluding debrief. The compact interface and narrative were part of its teaching design, not just decoration. Contemporary launch material and later classroom reports are the record; they are not code or artwork licenses.

| Status | What we know |
| --- | --- |
| Documented | Guided choices, simulated social metrics, a mobile-first interface and a final explanation; authentic material was used in the historical game. |
| Interpretation | A short sequence of authored decisions and visible consequences could reproduce the teaching rhythm, without reproducing the old script or scoring. |
| Unknown | Full branch graph, score formula, exact pacing, implementation, license and assets. We make no attempt to recover those. |

## What this project retains and changes

Retain immediacy, clear choices and consequences. Replace the operational role and authentic disturbing materials with a fictional editor evaluating evidence and deciding whether to publish, qualify, hold, correct or update. No mechanisms connect to external platforms. The emphasis is a trustworthy public record, not online influence.
